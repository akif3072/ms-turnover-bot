💰 EARNING RULES

MONEY STUDIO Turnover Reward Program

Minimum Requirements

Minimum qualifying balance: $10

Minimum turnover target: Balance × 50

Reward calculation: Completed turnover × 10%

Example — $20 Balance

$1,000 turnover → $100 reward

$2,000 turnover → $200 reward

$10,000 turnover → $1,000 reward

⚠️ Important Withdrawal Rule

Any withdrawal resets your program turnover progress to $0.

Your qualifying balance is reduced by the withdrawal amount. If it falls below $10, you are no longer eligible until it meets the minimum requirement again.

Additional Information

Trading profits or losses do not change your qualifying balance.

Additional deposits increase your qualifying balance and turnover target.

Rewards shown are estimates and subject to MONEY STUDIO program terms and verification.
